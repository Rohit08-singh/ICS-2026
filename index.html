ABOUT ICS 2026

Building India's Future Through Energy Innovation & Sustainability

The International Climate Summit (ICS) serves as a global platform where governments, policymakers, researchers, innovators and industry leaders collaborate to accelerate climate action and sustainable development.

The summit promotes renewable energy, climate resilience, food security, emerging technologies and strategic partnerships that support India's vision for a greener, more sustainable future.
