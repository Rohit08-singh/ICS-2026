About ICS 2026

The 6th International Climate Summit (ICS 2026) is India's flagship platform bringing together policymakers, government officials, industry leaders, technology providers, researchers, startups and global experts to accelerate India's transition towards a secure, resilient and sustainable energy future.

Through strategic partnerships, technology transfer, investment collaboration and policy dialogue, the summit transforms ideas into actionable solutions supporting India's Energy & Food Security Mission while strengthening climate resilience.

CONFERENCE FOCUS

Key Focus Areas of ICS 2026

Four strategic pillars driving India's Energy & Food Security Mission and enabling a sustainable future.

01
Renewable Energy

Bio Refineries & Bio Fuels

Accelerating India's clean energy transition through sustainable bio-refineries, advanced biofuels, green chemicals and circular bioeconomy initiatives for long-term energy security.

  • Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)
  • Second Generation Ethanol
  • Green Chemicals & Bio Plastics
  • Circular Bio Economy
Explore Bio Energy
02
Clean Coal Technologies

Coal Gasification

Unlocking the value of domestic coal resources by converting them into cleaner fuels, fertilizers, hydrogen and value-added chemicals while reducing environmental impact.

  • Hydrogen Production
  • Clean Chemical Manufacturing
  • Energy Security Solutions
  • Carbon Utilization Technologies
Discover Coal Innovation
04
AI & Industrial Safety

Process Safety & Artificial Intelligence

Integrating Artificial Intelligence, predictive analytics and digital transformation to enhance industrial safety, operational excellence and sustainable manufacturing practices.

  • Predictive Risk Analytics
  • Digital Safety Monitoring
  • AI Driven Process Control
  • Smart Industrial Operations
Explore AI Solutions
03
Nuclear Innovation

Small Modular Reactors

Exploring next-generation nuclear technologies that provide safe, reliable and low-carbon power to support India's growing industrial and energy requirements.

  • Low Carbon Electricity
  • Advanced Reactor Design
  • Industrial Energy Supply
  • Future Nuclear Technologies
